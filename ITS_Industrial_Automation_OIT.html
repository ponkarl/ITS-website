Who we are

ITS Industrial Technology Supply Co., Ltd. is Thailand's authorized distributor and representative of leading global industrial automation brands. Founded in 1993 and headquartered in Samutsakhon, we serve Thailand's industrial sector with best-quality, highest-reliability industrial grade products at affordable cost.

Our mission

Perfection in Automation — we bring world-class technology brands to Thailand's industrial market, providing local stock availability, technical expertise and after-sales support. We are committed to being the most reliable automation partner for engineers and procurement teams across Thailand.

What we supply

PLC & HMI systems, flow computers, DIN-rail power supplies, surge & lightning protection, 3G/4G industrial routers, Datacom and SCADA software — all available ex-stock from our Samutsakhon warehouse for immediate delivery anywhere in Thailand.

Who we serve

We serve system integrators, OEM machine builders, EPC contractors, plant engineers and procurement teams across oil & gas, manufacturing, energy, telecommunications, transportation and smart infrastructure sectors throughout Thailand and the region.
